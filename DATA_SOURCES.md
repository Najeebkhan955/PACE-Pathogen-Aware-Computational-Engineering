# Data sources, provenance and reuse

The [source provenance table](DATA_SOURCE_PROVENANCE.csv) identifies original published projects, author-supplied inputs and comparison data, including AMPlify, GRAMPA, HC50 input, AMPScanner v2, QMAP, HydrAMP, and reconstructed AMPGAN v2 results.

[Scientific provenance and licensing notes](docs/04_ATTRIBUTION_AND_LICENSES.md) identify upstream links and caution about third-party redistribution rights. No universal permissive license is claimed for the combined deposit.

## Data interpretation

- GRAMPA/other source MIC records and HC50 source measurements are **measured assay observations**, not measurements on the 20 designed PACE leads.
- Results of ESM-2, GA/NSGA-II and external-generator evaluations include model predictions/reconstructions and their original detailed caveats.
- Complete numerical records and checksums are available via S01–S12 directories; large binary archives in S06 and S10 are stored as byte-preserving parts.
- Original reviewer correspondence and manuscript drafts are intentionally excluded from this public deposit.

See [archive reassembly guide](docs/03_REPRODUCIBILITY.md) and [full folder index](SUPPLEMENTARY_INDEX.csv).
